# Executive Summary

Officially, WARDOGS launched with three maps, each with three variations, and a randomised 2x2 km Control Zone inside a larger battlefield [1][2]. No official source names the maps, profiles them, or explains how they rotate. This reference collects the per-map profiles that exist, all of which are player-reported: Bakurani, Ozeti and Zestafona, plus the unconfirmed Jutari and Saburtulo [3][4][5][6][7].

Several community sources agree on the three names, their settings and the existence of roughly three rotating areas per map. They disagree on play-area size, control-zone counts and whether a fourth map exists. Rain is the only announced Season 02 environment change [8].

Confidence is Low: the core claims are player-reported and the sources conflict. Nothing here adds official evidence to the foundation document.

# Key Takeaways

- Official: three maps, three variations each, as of the 10 Sep 2026 launch [2]. Map names are not given on the Steam page [1].
- Player-reported: the maps are Bakurani (Kavkazi), Ozeti (Europe) and Zestafona (North America) [3][4][6].
- Player-reported: each match uses one of about three rotating "areas" per map; each map lists a Default area plus two others [3][5][7].
- Conflict: the official Control Zone is 2x2 km [1]; allthings.how describes 500 m radius play circles (550 m on Ozeti) with an 85 m capture ring [3][6]. Not reconciled.
- Conflict: control-zone counts range from one ring per map [3] to 171 named zones across all maps [4] to three or four positions per map [5].
- Jutari (Sniper Showdown, 350 m) and Saburtulo are listed by one site only and are not confirmed playable [3].
- Season 02 (15 Oct 2026) adds rain as a per-map weather state, not a mid-match event; no new map was mentioned [8].

# Scope

Covers per-map profiles for the community-listed maps, the unconfirmed maps, rotation, and source conflicts, at Season 01 patch 0.1.2 with 2 Oct 2026 hotfixes. Excludes the general map overview, size and destructibility discussion (see maps-environment-foundation), Control Zone scoring rules (see gameplay-modes-foundation), and vehicle and outpost use (see vehicles-outposts-foundation). Every named detail below is player-reported unless marked official.

# Definitions

- **Control Zone**: the official randomised 2x2 km objective area inside a larger map [1].
- **Play area (community)**: a named circle at a defined position on a map where a match is fought; community term [3].
- **Hot Zone**: a smaller roaming sub-zone inside the Control Zone; community sources say it counts double presence [6][7]. Not profiled here.
- **Drill tower**: a capturable tower with a key code, per community guides [6][7].

# Current State

Verified 2026-10-08 against patch 0.1.2.

- Official: three maps with three variations each; "large-scale maps with dynamic objective zones"; Eastern European derelict industrial mountains; Control Zones are randomised 2x2 km areas within larger maps (Sep 2026) [1][2].
- Official sources do not name any map or give a map size [1][2].
- Player-reported: the three playable maps are Bakurani, Ozeti and Zestafona [3][4][5][7].
- Player-reported: allthings.how lists Bakurani Outskirts (Jutari) and Saburtulo as not confirmed playable; its introduction mentions "four playable battlegrounds" while its detailed sections list three [3].
- Press quote of Bulkhead: Season 02 rain is "an additional weather state per map and not a dynamic event mid match"; Season 02 is dated 15 Oct 2026 [8].

## Typical Control Zone behaviour (player-reported)

- allthings.how: one objective ring of 85 m radius at the centre of the live play area; on Zestafona the three live areas are picked at equal frequency [3][6]. On Bakurani it says the Default area is picked twice as often as the other two [3]; no other source says this.
- allthings.how: the farthest area centres are 285 m apart on Bakurani, 693 m on Ozeti and 531 m on Zestafona [3][6]. These distances fit a 500 m circle model, not a 2x2 km zone.
- Hot Zone: roams inside the Control Zone and counts double [6][7]. Boostroom says Bulkhead confirms double cash for it; I could not trace that to an official page [7].
- Terrain effect: Bakurani high ground over the valley road decides engagements [3]; Ozeti open approaches to the town are exposed [3][7]; Zestafona has long exposed approaches in the yards [3][7].

## Unconfirmed maps

| Map | Claim | Source |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Bakurani Outskirts, Jutari | Sniper Showdown map on the Kavkazi world; 350 m play circle, 75 m objective ring, single fixed area, no rotation; status "not confirmed as playable" | [3] |
| Saburtulo | Listed as a King of the Hill map under Russia; no play area or objective; not playable | [3] |

No other source read mentions either name; the guides from xgamingserver.com, gamebrief.net and boostroom.com name only three maps [4][5][7]. Treat both as unconfirmed.

## Rotation

- Official: no rotation is documented. Bulkhead says only that the Control Zone is randomised [1].
- Player-reported: each map has three live areas drawn from a defined list of three or four; allthings.how says selection on Zestafona is equally likely [3][6]. Gamebrief says the whole map is not confirmed to reroll each match and no map vote or map order is documented [5].
- Assessment (Low): the "three variations" per map [2] plausibly match the three live areas, but no source states this.

## Season 02 weather

Rain is announced as one extra weather state per map, set for the whole match; it does not change mid match, and the effect on visibility or gameplay was not stated [8]. The coverage mentions no new map [8]. Nothing else about the Season 02 environment is taken as fact here.

# Risks & Caveats

- Almost every figure is player-reported; several publishers sell hosting or boosting services (xgamingserver.com, boostroom.com), a conflict of interest.
- Guide sites may draw on one another, so agreement may not be independent. Assessment (Low): agreement on names is not verification.
- Early Access: areas, rotation and weather can change, and Season 02 lands 15 Oct 2026.
- Steam news post bodies do not render to fetchers, so official patch text could not be read.
- A search summary said one guide version marks Jutari as playable; I rely only on the fetched wording [3].
