# Executive Summary

Official posts confirm which kinds of action pay cash (revives, transport, kills, spotting, holding the zone), that the Hot Zone pays double cash, and that cash persists between matches [4][5]. They publish no per-action dollar amounts. The only official reward figures found are a temporary 5% end-of-match cash bonus on community servers (14 Sep 2026) and the Season 1 XP-event changes [2][3].

What changed for rewards is documented officially: Update 0.1.2 made revive validity server-authoritative, capped vehicle fly-through rewards, scaled buildable-destruction rewards by build progress and reduced rewards for FOB pallet deposits outside the zone, while a ban policy for economy abuse took effect [1][2]. None of those changes is quantified.

This is a pre-Season-02 baseline (Season 02 is teased for 15 Oct 2026 [7]). Confidence is Medium: the structure of the reward system is official, but amounts are unpublished or rest on player-reported guides.

# Key Takeaways

- "Every teamplay action you perform rewards cash" and the Hot Zone earns "double the cash" (Steam page, 2026-10-08) [5].
- No official per-action cash amount was found as of 2026-10-08; any figure in a guide is player-reported [4][11].
- Update 0.1.2 (30 Sep 2026) capped vehicle fly-through rewards, scaled buildable-destruction rewards to build progress and reduced out-of-zone FOB pallet rewards, with no values stated [1].
- Patch 0.11 (14 Sep 2026) fixed "various cash exploits" and gave community servers a one-week 5% end-of-match cash bonus [2].
- Cash exploit abuse means a timed ban plus cash and XP reset to 0; tutorialising exploits or XP farming means a permanent ban [2]; from 0.1.2 first-offence leniency is over [1].
- Season 1 (10 Sep 2026) changed prices and XP, not documented per-action cash (for example air-vehicle player kill 300 to 250 XP) [3].
- Cash can be exchanged for Gold Bars per an official February 2026 FAQ [4].

# Scope

Covers how cash is earned and lost per action, the Hot Zone multiplier, teamplay rewards, exploit fixes in 0.11 and 0.1.2, and Season 1 economy changes compared with the closed beta, as of 0.1.2 plus the 2 Oct hotfix. Excludes the item price tables (see price-tables-season-1), the death penalty and rebuy rules (death-penalty-and-rebuy), the seasonal reset and Gold Bar rate (season-reset-and-currencies) and match flow (control-zone-and-match-flow). It deepens economy-foundation without restating it.

# Definitions

- **Teamplay action**: an action that the developer says rewards cash, such as reviving, transporting or spotting [4].
- **Hot Zone**: a shifting sub-zone of the Control Zone where players count double towards presence and earn double cash [4].
- **End-of-match bonus**: a cash bonus at match end, referenced by the 5% community-server promotion [2].
- **Economy abuse**: cash or XP exploits, tutorialising them, XP farming or real money trading as defined in the developer policy [1][2].

# Current State

- Teamplay actions award cash; the official examples are reviving squadmates, transporting friendlies to the zone and controlling the objective (Steam page, 2026-10-08) [5].
- The Hot Zone is described as yielding double cash, and players count double towards zone presence (Steam news, 18 Feb 2026) [4].
- Cash persists between matches and can be spent or exchanged for Gold Bars, the endgame cosmetic currency (18 Feb 2026) [4].
- In-game cash and Gold Bars will never be sold for real money (11 Aug 2026) [6].
- Reward integrity changes in Update 0.1.2 are live (30 Sep 2026) [1].

# Detail

## Earning actions

| Source class | Actions listed | Amount |
|--------------|----------------|--------|
| Official (Steam news, 18 Feb 2026) | Revives, transport, kills, spotting, "and MORE" [4] | Not published |
| Official (Steam page) | Reviving squadmates, transporting friendlies to the zone, controlling the objective [5] | Not published |
| Official (0.1.2 notes) | Revive actions, vehicle fly-through, destroying buildables, depositing pallets into a FOB [1] | Not published |
| Player-reported (allthings.how) | Kills, assists, revives, heals, spotting, resupplying, repairing vehicles, FOB building, hauling supplies, driving or flying teammates, standing on the objective, teammate tips, holding the Control Zone, destroying enemy vehicles (loaded helicopters labelled high payout) [11] | Not published |

The player-reported guide's own dollar figures are limited to the $10,000 starting balance [11]. A second guide gives only item costs (for example Galil unlock $35,000, Defibrillator unlock $50,000 plus $1,600 per loadout purchase, Basic Parachute $100), which are costs, not income (player-reported) [12].

## Hot Zone double cash

Official: the Hot Zone is a shifting sub-zone inside the Control Zone, players inside count double for presence and it "yields DOUBLE CASH" [4]. The Steam page repeats "chase the 'Hot Zone' to earn double the cash" [5]. Player-reported: standard cash applies in the Control Zone outside the Hot Zone, and the Hot Zone is a risk-for-payout trade, with no payout figures given [13]. Whether the multiplier applies to every action or only some is not stated officially (see Open Questions).

## Exploit and abuse policy

| Offence | Official consequence | Date |
|---------|----------------------|------|
| Abusing cash exploits and bugs | Timed ban scaled to prior bans; cash and XP reset to 0 [2] | 14 Sep 2026 |
| Creating content that tutorialises exploits | Treated as advertising cheats; permanent ban [2] | 14 Sep 2026 |
| XP farming (joining farming servers more than a few minutes) | Permanent ban [2] | 14 Sep 2026 |
| Repeatable unintended exploits, real money trading | Cash and progression reset; risk of permanent ban without warning; first-offence leniency ended [1] | 30 Sep 2026 |

The developer states that testing an exploit "once or twice" is acceptable, but repeating it is abuse [2].

The Season 1.0 changelog (10 Sep 2026) states the previous balance "was designed specifically for weekend long beta tests" and that each season will have a different XP curve and item order [3]. # Early Access Changes & Known Issues

- 10 Sep 2026, Season 1.0: price and XP rebalance, supply-crate curve rescaled "so low value items are priced sensibly and cannot be abused" [3].
- 14 Sep 2026, Patch 0.11: "Various cash exploits have been fixed"; economy abuse policy; 5% community-server end-of-match bonus for one week [2].
- 30 Sep 2026, Update 0.1.2: revive, fly-through, buildable and pallet reward fixes; zero-tolerance statement [1].
- 2 Oct 2026 hotfix: IR Rangefinders disabled and CIWS tweak; no reward change [8].
- Planned: Season 02 on 15 Oct 2026 [7]; the developer calls its economy interlinked "even more so when we bring the meta into the game" [2], and a Black Market, Vault and challenge system are planned for Early Access [9].

# Admin & Technical Notes

The 5% bonus applied to community servers only and was a developer promotion, not a server setting [2]. None found for per-action reward tuning.
