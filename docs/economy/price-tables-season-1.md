# Executive Summary

This is the pre-Season-02 baseline of every price the studio itself has published for WARDOGS. The only official in-game price list is the "Economy Changes" section of the Season 1 changelog posted on Steam on 9 Sep 2026 (live 10 Sep), which states "Every price change in one place" and gives 15 repriced items, each with an old and a new value [1]. No later official post (0.11, 0.1.2, the 2 Oct hotfix) states a new item price [2][3][4]. Store prices come from the Steam store data snapshot [5][6].

Important limit: the changelog lists only items whose price changed. Prices of unchanged items are not published in any official source found, so this is a complete list of published changes, not a complete shop catalogue. Season 02 is due 15 Oct 2026 and the studio says each season gets a different XP curve and item order [1], so these values are expected to be superseded.

Confidence is High because every value is quoted from an official Steam post or the Steam store data; no player-reported figures are used.

# Key Takeaways

- 15 item prices were changed in the Season 1.0 changelog, 10 Sep 2026; each has an old and new value in the tables below [1].
- Kodiak Flatbed went UP: $15,000 to $35,000 per the official text (Season 1.0) [1]. This resolves the direction conflict (see Open Questions).
- Largest rises: Career Artillery Tank +$100,000, Support Large Hammer +$50,000, Pilot Little Bird with miniguns +$25,000 [1].
- Reductions: Pilot Z20 Lakota and Driver URAL (both -$15,000), Driver Dune Buggy (-$10,000), Driver Music Tape H (-$2,500) [1].
- The supply-crate purchase curve was rescaled from 25-250 to 1-200 (Season 1.0) [1].
- No official post after 10 Sep publishes a new item price; Patch 0.11 only added a one-week 5% end-of-match cash bonus on community servers [2][3][4].
- Steam store: Standard $39.99 / GBP 36.99, Supporter Edition $49.99 / GBP 45.99, Supporter Pack DLC $12.99 / GBP 11.99 (snapshot 2026-10-08) [5][6].

# Scope

Covers every price and price-like number in official Steam posts and Steam store data up to 8 Oct 2026: vendor and track item prices, supply-crate pricing, store prices and the cash bonus. Also records, for later diffing, the XP bands, XP events and unlock-level moves printed in the same changelog. Excludes unchanged item prices (unpublished), per-action cash rewards (see cash-rewards-and-costs), weapon stats (see weapons-catalogue), gadget detail (see gadgets-armor-and-equipment) and vehicle behaviour (see vehicle-roster-and-prices). Parent: economy-foundation.

# Definitions

- **Old / New**: the values before and after the Season 1.0 launch changelog, as written by the studio [1].
- **Track**: the role or career progression line under which an item is unlocked and listed (Career, Recon, Support, Pilot, Driver, Vendor) [1].
- **Baseline value**: a number stamped with the version it was true for, so Season 02 changes can be diffed mechanically.

# Current State

As of Season 1.0 plus patches to 2 Oct 2026, the Season 1 changelog is the latest official statement of every repriced item below [1]. The studio says the Forward Operating Base and Large Hammer vendor rises make them "more of a choice" [1]. IR Rangefinders were disabled in the vendor from 2 Oct 2026 until Season 02, where they will require batteries; no price was stated [4]. Update 0.1.2 capped vehicle fly-through rewards, scaled buildable-destruction rewards by build progress and reduced FOB deposit rewards outside the zone, without publishing amounts [3].

# Early Access Changes & Known Issues

- 10 Sep 2026, Season 1.0: all price changes above [1].
- 14 Sep 2026, Patch 0.11: one-week 5% end-of-match cash bonus on community servers; economy abuse policy [2].
- 30 Sep 2026, Update 0.1.2: reward caps and reductions for exploits; no price table [3].
- 2 Oct 2026 hotfix: IR Rangefinders removed from the vendor until Season 02, where they will need batteries; no price stated [4].
- Season 02 (due 15 Oct 2026) will use a different XP curve and item order [1]; re-diff every table here against its notes.

# Admin & Technical Notes

None found. The changelog does not state whether prices are configurable on community servers.

# Risks & Caveats

- Unchanged items are absent because no official source lists them; do not read absence as price zero.
- Old values come from the studio's own changelog text, not from a first-hand capture of the pre-Season-1 shop.
- Prices are expected to change at Season 02 [1].
- Store prices vary by region and currency; only USD and GBP were captured [5][6].

# Open Questions

- Kodiak Flatbed direction: the official Season 1 text says $15,000 to $35,000, an increase [1], and the economy-foundation table agrees. The foundation also notes a press outlet describing it as a reduction; that report is not cited here and the official text takes precedence. Confidence in the official figure: High.
- Unit and meaning of the supply-crate curve "25 to 250 down to 1 to 200": not stated [1].
- Prices of unchanged items (for example the base Bobcat and most weapons): no official source; see vehicle-roster-and-prices and weapons-catalogue for community-labelled data.
- Whether the 5% community-server bonus ended on schedule: no official follow-up found [2].
