# Executive Summary

This is the pre-wipe baseline for seasons and currencies, taken on 2026-10-08, one week before Season 02 (15 October 2026). Official Steam posts confirm that cash persists from match to match, that cash can be exchanged for Gold Bars, that Gold Bars are the "endgame currency" for cosmetic unlocks in a "Gold Market", that Gold Bars and cash will never be sold for real money, and that each season gets its own XP curve and item order [2][3][4]. The Season 02 teaser confirms only the date [1].

The explicit rule that a season end resets cash and XP, auto-converts leftover cash to Gold Bars, and keeps Gold Bars and cosmetics permanently is NOT in any Steam post in the local snapshot. It is attributed to a 7 April 2026 BULKHEAD devlog and is relayed by Vice, KeenGamer and Timesaver.gg [10][11][12]. The earlier single-source Timesaver claim is therefore half-confirmed: the existence of Gold Bars and the cash-to-Gold exchange are officially confirmed; the automatic end-of-season conversion and permanence rest on a devlog the press quotes but this KB could not read. The conversion rate is unpublished everywhere.

Confidence is Medium: the currency model is official, the wipe mechanics are second-hand.

# Key Takeaways

- Official: "Cash earned persists in your account from match to match", and cash can be exchanged "for GOLD BARS - the endgame currency used to obtain cosmetic unlocks in the GOLD MARKET" (Steam post, 18 Feb 2026) [2].
- Official: BULKHEAD "will NEVER monetize in-game cash or gold bars" and will never allow direct purchase of camos (Steam post, 11 Aug 2026) [3].
- Official: "Each season we will have a different XP curve with different item orders" (Season 1 changelog, 9 Sep 2026) [4].
- Official: Season 02 starts 15 October 2026; the teaser gives no time and no wipe detail [1].
- Official: Season 02 is "a larger content update, with new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks and additional features" (0.1.2 post, 30 Sep 2026) [5].
- Press (quoting a devlog not in the snapshot): at season end cash and XP reset, leftover cash auto-converts to Gold Bars, and Gold Bars and cosmetics persist permanently [10][11][12].
- Not published anywhere found: the cash-to-Gold-Bar rate, Season 02 prices, Season 02 XP curve, and whether the starting cash is again $10,000 [11][12].
- Official: exploit sanctions already reset cash and XP to 0 for individuals, a separate mechanism from the seasonal reset [5][6].

# Definitions

- **Season**: a content and progression cycle; "Season 1.0" is the label for the 10 Sep 2026 launch changelog [4].
- **Cash**: persistent in-game currency, earned by teamplay and spent on loadouts [2][9].
- **Gold Bars**: the "endgame currency" obtained by exchanging cash, spent on cosmetics [2].
- **Gold Market**: the named store for cosmetic unlocks bought with Gold Bars [2].
- **Wipe / reset**: loss of cash and XP progression; in this KB "seasonal reset" is the press-reported end-of-season event, distinct from the exploit-sanction reset [5][10].

# Current State

As of 2026-10-08, pre-wipe:

- Season 1 is live (build 0.1.2 plus hotfixes); Season 02 is teased for 15 October 2026 [1][5][8].
- Cash persists between matches [2][9].
- Cash can be exchanged for Gold Bars; Gold Bars buy cosmetics in the Gold Market [2].
- Neither cash nor Gold Bars is ever to be sold, and camos are not to be purchasable directly [3].
- The only real-money item is the optional Supporter Edition (cosmetics) [3][9].
- The studio frames progression as "continue earning, progressing and adding value to your account even when not in a match" via a planned Metagame, including a Black Market and Vault and a Skills and Challenge system, built during Early Access [7].
- The 22 Sep teaser contains only "WARDOGS Season 02 is coming. 15th October 2026. Stay tuned for more details." [1]
- The 30 Sep post promised more information "next week" [5]; no further official Season 02 post existed in the snapshot at 2026-10-08.

## Gold Bars: the Timesaver claim

Timesaver.gg claims leftover cash converts to Gold Bars and that Gold Bars and cosmetics are "the only things that survive" the wipe, and sells in-game cash [12]. Assessment: partly confirmed, confidence Medium. The Gold Bar currency, the cash exchange and the cosmetic role are in an official Steam post [2], and Vice and KeenGamer independently relay the same end-of-season rule [10][11]. The part still without a readable official text is the automatic conversion and permanence; Timesaver cites the 7 April devlog for it [12]. The official 7 April Steam post only announces the Metagame and links a video [7]. Timesaver also has a commercial interest in cash, so its "spend first" advice and rate remarks are not evidence.

Rain: Vice reports BULKHEAD said on X that rain is an additional per-map weather state, not a dynamic mid-match event [10]. Not economy, noted because it travels with the same reset coverage.

## Exploit-reset precedent

Separate from seasons, the 0.11 and 0.1.2 posts state that exploiters receive a timed ban and "We will reset your cash and XP to 0", and that anyone caught with economy exploits or real-money trading "has had their cash and progression reset" [5][6].

- 11 Aug 2026: no-monetisation pledges on cash and Gold Bars [3].

# Risks & Caveats

- This is a snapshot taken a week before the wipe; Season 02 notes will supersede the Baseline values.
- The reset rule is press-relayed from a video devlog; wording may differ from BULKHEAD's original.
- The Gold Bar exchange appears only in a February pre-launch FAQ; behaviour may have changed.
- Commercial sources (Timesaver) are conflicted and used only for labelled claims.
- Team17 itself made no seasonal statement found; the statements are BULKHEAD's.

# Open Questions

- Does the end-of-season reset happen at Season 02 start? Press says "expect one"; no official post says so. Resolve with the Season 02 patch notes.
- What is the cash-to-Gold-Bar rate, and is it fixed or variable? Unpublished; the "fluctuates daily" claim is unsourced [12].
- Official text contradiction: the FAQ says players can "exchange" cash for Gold Bars (a player action) [2], while press describes automatic conversion at season end [10][11]. Both may be true; unresolved.
- Does the Season 1 pledge "gold bars... must be earned through play" [3] conflict with any later Gold Bar source? None found.
- Do Supporter Edition cosmetics persist? Not stated.
- Foundation conflict: economy-foundation lists the wipe as player-reported only; this document upgrades part of it (Gold Bars and exchange) to official [2].
