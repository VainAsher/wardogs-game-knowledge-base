# Executive Summary

This document is a pre-Season 02 baseline of WARDOGS progression: the role-track XP curve, XP event values, the unlock-level moves, the 0.1.2 exploit fixes and what is officially said about seasons. It is written from the full text of the official Steam posts saved in the 2026-10-08 snapshot, which supersede the press summaries used in the foundation documents.

The official Season 1 changelog (posted 9 Sep 2026) names six role tracks: Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver and Pilot [1]. It gives an XP band table from level 6 to level 50 (level 50 costs 1,000,000 XP), says the old values were tuned for weekend-long beta tests, and says each season will have a different XP curve and item order [1]. The Season 02 teaser contains only a date, 15 October 2026, and says nothing about XP [3]. The official posts do not state a level cap or any WARDOG level; those come from a player-reported guide [9].

Confidence is Medium. The XP bands, event values and unlock moves rest on an official post (High on their own). The level cap, the WARDOG level formula and the seasonal wipe details rest on player-reported or press sources, which holds the document at Medium.

# Key Takeaways

- Six role tracks are named in official text: Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot (Season 1 changelog, 9 Sep 2026) [1]. The name "Assault" used by guides and earlier KB documents does not appear in the official text I read [9].
- The XP curve was deliberately slowed versus the beta: level 10 from 25,000 to 35,000 XP, level 20 from 111,500 to 250,000 and level 50 from 838,000 to 1,000,000 [1].
- Developer target: a median player at 20 to 25 hours will be around level 20, and the top 5 percent chasing the big-ticket items "will need to grind" [1].
- Two XP event values changed: a player kill using an air vehicle 300 to 250 XP, and a supply crate item sold under $75 from 25 XP to 1 XP [1].
- "Each season we will have a different XP curve with different item orders" is an official statement, so every number in this document is a Season 1 value [1].
- Patch 0.1.2 (30 Sep 2026) fixed several cash and XP exploits and ended first-offence leniency: abusers have cash and progression reset and risk a permanent ban [2].
- The Season 02 teaser (22 Sep 2026) gives only the date 15 October 2026 [3]. A wipe of cash and level progression is reported by press from earlier developer statements, but I found no official post confirming it in the snapshot [8].

# Definitions

- **Role track**: a per-role XP and level track that gates which items can be bought. The official changelog names the tracks Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver and Pilot [1].
- **XP band**: a level at which the changelog publishes a cumulative XP requirement [1].
- **WARDOG level**: an account-wide level, described by a player-reported guide as the sum of the six track levels [9]. Not defined in any official post I read.
- **Season**: a period after which, per press, cash and level progression reset; the developer says each season has its own XP curve [1][8].

# Current State

- Role tracks (Season 1 changelog, 9 Sep 2026): the changelog uses Career, Medic, Support and Driver as headings for unlock moves, and Career, Recon, Support, Pilot and Driver as prefixes on price changes. Together that is six track names: Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot [1].
- XP curve: the changelog says it "rebalanced the role track XP curve from level 6 upward" and publishes bands for levels 6, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50, with levels below 6 unchanged [1].
- Developer intent: levels slowed around 10 to 20, curve flattened from 35 onward, median players at 20 to 25 hours around level 20, top 5 percent needing to grind [1].
- Season 02 is dated 15 October 2026 and the teaser carries no XP detail [3]. The 0.1.2 post says Season 02 "is planned to be a larger content update, with new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks and additional features" [2].
- The developer has said it will never monetise in-game cash or Gold Bars, which "must be earned through play" (pre-order post, 11 Aug 2026) [5].
- Exploit policy: cash and XP reset to 0 and timed bans for cash exploits, permanent bans for XP farming (patch 0.11 post, 12 Sep 2026) [4]; reaffirmed and tightened in 0.1.2 (30 Sep 2026) [2].

# Detail

## Role tracks and the 4-versus-6 and naming conflicts

| Question | Official text | Other sources | Resolution |
|----------|---------------|---------------|------------|
| How many tracks? | Six names appear: Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot [1] | Six tracks, four levelled by weapons and tools, Driver and Pilot levelled by vehicles (player-reported) [9] | Six. The "four" is a count of weapon-based tracks, not a smaller set. |
| What is the first track called? | "Career" [1] | "Assault", levelled from assault rifles (player-reported) [9] | Official wording is Career. Whether Career is a rename of, or the same track as, the guide's Assault is not stated in the official post. Open question. |
| Does an official post call the tracks "roles"? | It says "role track" [1] | Guides say "roles" [9] | Use "role track". |

Evidence that Career is a full track and not a catch-all: the changelog lists Career unlocks (Sports Parachute, Large Backpack, 762x54mm AP, 556mm AP, Deagle, Artillery Tank) [1]. The changelog also records items moving between tracks: Music Tape H from Pilot to Driver, and Heavy Tank from Career to Driver [1].

## Season 1 curve versus beta

The changelog says the earlier balancing "was designed specifically for weekend long beta tests" and that the new values are "in-line with where we believe they should be" [1]. The "old" column below is therefore the beta-era curve, as stated by the developer.

| Level | Beta XP | Season 1 XP | Change as published | Derived: Season 1 XP added since previous band |
|-------|---------|-------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Below 6 | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged | n/a |
| 6 | 8,750 | 9,250 | 5.7% slower | n/a |
| 10 | 25,000 | 35,000 | 40.0% slower | 25,750 |
| 15 | 54,750 | 110,000 | 100.9% slower | 75,000 |
| 20 | 111,500 | 250,000 | 124.2% slower | 140,000 |
| 25 | 192,000 | 325,000 | 69.3% slower | 75,000 |
| 30 | 293,750 | 400,000 | 36.2% slower | 75,000 |
| 35 | 416,500 | 500,000 | 20.0% slower | 100,000 |
| 40 | 551,250 | 650,000 | 17.9% slower | 150,000 |
| 45 | 689,500 | 800,000 | 16.0% slower | 150,000 |
| 50 | 838,000 | 1,000,000 | 19.3% slower | 200,000 |

Source for the first four data columns: [1]. The last column is my subtraction of adjacent Season 1 values and is not in any source. Assessment: the headline says the slowdown is "around 10 to 20" with flattening from 35 onward, yet the published bands show every band to level 50 slower than beta by at least 16 percent; confidence High that this is what the table says, Low on what the developer meant by "flatten" (the post does not elaborate) [1]. The same section describes "a larger increase at level 10" than the 5.7 percent at level 6 [1].

Intermediate levels (for example 7 to 9, 11 to 14) have no published XP value; the post gives bands only [1]. Whether the figures are cumulative per track or per level is not spelled out; the XP-to-reach wording ("Level 10: 25,000 to 35,000") reads as cumulative requirement (Assessment: Medium confidence, based on the figures rising monotonically) [1].

## Level caps and WARDOG level

- Official: no level cap is stated. The highest levels named in the changelog are Career level 90 (Artillery Tank) and Career level 85 (Deagle), so a cap of at least 90 is implied (Assessment: High that the cap is 90 or more, from [1]).
- Player-reported: each track caps at level 100, and the account-wide WARDOG Level is "the sum of all six tracks" capped at 600 [9]. The arithmetic (6 x 100 = 600) is consistent with a cap of 100, but neither number is in an official post I read.
- Official XP stops at level 50 (1,000,000 XP); XP needed for levels 51 to 100 is not published [1].

## 0.1.2 XP and cash exploit fixes (30 Sep 2026)

All from the official 0.1.2 post [2]:

1. Revive action validity checks are fully server authoritative.
2. Vehicle fly-through exploit fixed; rewards capped.
3. Rewards for destroying buildables scale with build progress.
4. Depositing pallets into a FOB outside the zone awards reduced rewards.
5. Policy: anyone caught abusing cash and XP exploits or Real Money Trading "has had their cash and progression reset and risks being permanently banned"; first-time leniency is over; repeatable unintended exploits can bring a permanent ban without warning [2].

The earlier patch 0.11 post (12 Sep 2026) set the tiers: cash exploits get a timed ban and a cash and XP reset to 0; tutorials on exploits count as advertising cheats and get a permanent ban; XP farming "will result in the most serious bans" and joining a known XP-farming server for more than a few minutes risks a permanent ban [4]. The post gives no figure for the reduced or capped rewards [2].

## What the Season 02 teaser says about XP

The official teaser (22 Sep 2026) reads, in full, that Season 02 is coming, dated 15 October 2026, with "more details" to follow, plus a trailer link [3]. It says nothing about XP, levels or resets. What is known about XP in Season 02 comes from elsewhere:

- Official, indirect: "Each season we will have a different XP curve with different item orders" (Season 1 changelog) [1]. Season 02 is "a larger content update, with new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks and additional features" [2].
- Press: Vice (23 Sep 2026) says players "should expect a progression wipe", that Bulkhead "has previously explained that cash and level progression reset at the end of a season", that leftover cash converts to Gold Bars and that Gold Bars and cosmetics persist; it also says the new unlock order and conversion rate are not yet shared [8].
- Player-reported: a guide says WARDOG Level and cash "drop to zero" when a major update cycle ends [9].

# Risks & Caveats

- This is a baseline that Season 02 (15 Oct 2026) is expected to replace; the developer says curves and item orders differ per season [1].
- The cap, WARDOG level and wipe details are player-reported or press-reported only [8][9].
- Percentages in the table are the developer's own figures; the derived column is my subtraction.
- The changelog prose says levels 10 to 20 were slowed, while its band table shows slowdowns to level 50 [1].

# Open Questions

- Career vs Assault: the foundation and combat-loadouts documents use "Assault" from a guide; the official changelog says "Career". Resolve by checking the in-game track list or a later official post [1][9].
- Level cap: not in any official post read. Resolve in game or via Season 02 notes [9].
- WARDOG level: no official definition; the sum-of-tracks formula and 600 cap are from one player-reported guide [9].
- Season 02 wipe: the foundation cites a boosting-service blog for the reset; the official posts in the snapshot do not state a reset of levels or the Gold Bar conversion rate. Vice cites earlier developer statements [8]. Resolve by locating the original developer post.
- XP values for kills, objectives and other actions, and the exploit reward caps, are unpublished [2].
- Official-versus-foundation difference: the foundation says "Assault or Career" and level 100 and 600 caps; the official text supports only Career, with no cap [1].
- Whether community-server play earns progression XP, and the per-level XP between bands, are not stated.
