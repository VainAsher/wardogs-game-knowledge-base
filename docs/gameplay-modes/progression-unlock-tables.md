# Executive Summary

This is a reference-data baseline of the Season 1 unlock levels, prices and XP bands that the developer published, captured the week before Season 02, which the developer dates to 15 Oct 2026 [5]. It reads the full text of the official Season 1.0 changelog Steam post (9 Sep 2026) [1] and checks the later official posts for any further unlock change [2][3][4].

The headline limit: the official post is a list of changes, not a full unlock list. It gives the new unlock level for 18 items, new prices for 14 items, and XP bands. It does not publish a complete level-by-level table for each of the six role tracks or for the account level. This document tables exactly what is official and does not fill gaps from guides [1].

Confidence is Medium. Every number in the tables is official [1], but coverage is partial, the post does not say which price rows are one-time unlock fees versus per-life vendor prices, and the developer says each season has a different XP curve and item order [1].

# Key Takeaways

- Official source: 18 unlock-level moves, 14 price moves and 10 XP bands are published for Season 1.0 (9 Sep 2026); no full per-role list exists in the official text read [1].
- Six progression tracks are official [6]; the changelog names Career, Medic, Support and Driver in its unlock headings, with Recon and Pilot appearing in its price lines [1].
- Largest moves: Artillery Tank from Career 55 to 90 and Heavy Tank from Career 35 to Driver 35 [1].
- XP curve: level 20 went from 111,500 to 250,000 XP; level 50 from 838,000 to 1,000,000 XP [1].
- No unlock level changed in patch 0.11, patch 0.1.2 or the 2 Oct hotfixes; the IR Rangefinder was pulled from the vendor until Season 02 [2][3][4].
- The developer states each season has a different XP curve and different item order, so these tables are a Season 1 snapshot [1].

# Scope

Covers the Season 1 unlock-level, price and XP-band values published in official Steam posts, with later patch changes noted. Game version: Season 1.0 as of 8 Oct 2026. Excluded: full weapon, vehicle and equipment descriptions (see weapons-catalogue, vehicle-roster-and-prices and gadgets-armor-and-equipment), the explanation of the track system (see progression-and-role-tracks), and any Season 02 values.

# Definitions

- **Track**: one of six role progression tracks that earn XP and gate unlocks [6].
- **Unlock level**: the track level at which an item becomes available [1].
- **Baseline**: the value as of the Season 1.0 changelog and later patches up to 8 Oct 2026, kept for mechanical diffing against Season 02.

See also `GLOSSARY.md`.

# Current State

All values below are official [1], stated for Season 1.0 (9 Sep 2026), and not changed by later official posts through 8 Oct 2026 unless noted [2][3][4].

- The changelog says levels 10 to 20 were slowed and the curve flattened from level 35 onward, so median players at 20 to 25 hours sit around level 20 [1].
- Season 1 unlock moves are listed under the headings Career, Medic, Support and Driver [1].
- Vendor prices for the Forward Operating Base and Large Hammer rose "to make them more of a choice" [1].
- Season 02 is dated 15 Oct 2026 in the developer teaser [5], and described as a larger content update with new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks and additional features [2].

# Risks & Caveats

- The tables are changes only; items never moved are absent, so the lists are not complete unlock tables [1].
- Season 02 is due 15 Oct 2026 and the developer says the XP curve and item order differ each season, so every row may be replaced [1][5].
- The post does not label price rows as unlock fee or per-life price (except "vendor"), so do not read the price column as one or the other [1].
- Player-reported guides in the KB give other levels for some items; they are not used for values here [7].

# Open Questions

- Official versus foundation doc: gameplay-modes-foundation says the official Steam announcement text could not be read and names the first role "Assault or Career"; the post is now read and names Career, so that wording is stale [1].
- Official versus foundation doc: gameplay-modes-foundation reports a player-reported level 100 track cap and account level 600; the official changelog gives XP bands only to level 50 and Career unlocks up to 90, and states no cap [1]. Resolution: an in-game progression screen or a developer post.
- Official versus foundation doc: gameplay-modes-foundation states a Season 02 reset of cash and XP, relayed by a third party; the official teaser and 0.1.2 post read here give the date and a larger content update but do not state a wipe [2][5].
- Official versus vehicles guides: a guide cited in vehicles-outposts-foundation puts Large Hammer at level 7, while the official post says it moved from 7 to 8 [1]. Official is taken as the Season 1.0 value.
- Are band XP figures per level or cumulative, and are the price rows unlock fees or per-life prices? Resolution: in-game check.
- Unlock levels for items not in the changelog, and account-level (WARDOG Level) unlocks, are not in the official text read.
