# Executive Summary

WARDOGS is a 100-player, three-team tactical shooter in Steam Early Access since 10 September 2026. Its core mode is the Control Zone: teams contest a randomised 2x2 km zone, the team with the most players inside scores, and the first to 100 points wins [1][3]. Local voice chat is heard across all three teams, and the Steam page describes it as proximity voice chat [1][3].

Progression is cash-based within and across matches, plus a role-track XP system that gates unlocks and resets each season [1][5][9]. The developer's stated 1.0 direction is a wider meta game and long-term systems, more maps, weapons and vehicles, deeper progression, a seasonal meta game and fighter jets [1][3]. Season 02 is announced for 15 October 2026 [7].

Confidence is Medium. The match format, voice chat, cash and Early Access plans rest on the Steam page and the Team17 press release. The role-track detail, hot-zone and tower mechanics, and the October patch contents rest on press and player-reported sources, because the official Steam announcement text could not be read directly in this session.

# Key Takeaways

- Format: up to 100 players in three teams; the Early Access launch set is three maps, each with three variations (verified 2026-10-08) [1][3].
- Objective: a randomised 2x2 km Control Zone; the team with the most players inside earns points; first to 100 wins; a Hot Zone gives double cash [1].
- Voice: local voice chat carries across all three teams, so enemies in earshot can be heard [3].
- Progression: cash carries between matches and every teamplay action pays [1]; XP also accrues to role tracks that gate unlocks (player-reported structure, see Detail) [5][9].
- Seasons: Season 01 ran from launch; Season 02 is announced for 15 Oct 2026 with a reset of cash and XP while Gold Bars and cosmetics persist [7].
- Modes: the Control Zone is the only official mode named at Early Access; community servers can run modified modes, and a Deploy flow steers new players to Infantry mode and low-level community servers as of 0.1.2 [3][4][6].
- 1.0: no date or dated roadmap has been given; Early Access is expected to last around one to two years [1][8].

# Definitions

- **Control Zone**: the randomised 2x2 km objective area in each match [1].
- **Hot Zone**: a smaller area where presence and cash count double [1].
- **Role track**: a per-role XP track (player-reported; see Detail) [9].
- **Season**: a period after which cash and XP progression reset [7].

See also `GLOSSARY.md`.

# Current State

All statements verified 2026-10-08 against the sources shown.

- Early Access began 10 Sep 2026; the developer describes it as fully playable and representing the core vision, expected to last around 1 to 2 years [1].
- Matches support up to 100 players split across three rival teams [1][3].
- Teams fight for a randomised 2x2 km Control Zone; the team with the most players in the zone earns points and the first to 100 wins [1].
- A Hot Zone offers double cash [1].
- Players start with $10,000 and buy a loadout each life; teamplay actions such as reviving, transporting teammates and holding the objective earn cash; cash carries over between matches [1].
- Local voice chat can be heard across all three teams [3]; the Steam page lists proximity voice chat [1].
- Launch content is three maps, each with three variations, with infantry and vehicle combat [3].
- Patch 0.11 (14 Sep 2026) split the server browser into Official and Community lists and added a 5% end-of-match cash bonus on Community servers for one week [4]. Note the French and German press number versions differently, so version labels vary by outlet [4][6].
- Patch 0.1.2 (30 Sep 2026) added server-side checks on revives, capped rewards for destroying buildables by construction progress, reduced rewards for depositing pallets outside designated zones, and added a Deploy flow toward Infantry mode and low-level community servers [6].
- The 2 Oct 2026 hotfixes are reported inconsistently: one outlet says no gameplay or balance changes [6], another reports IR rangefinders disabled until Season 02 and a faster CWIS kill on the Havoc [7].
- Season 02 is announced for 15 Oct 2026 via a developer Steam teaser dated 22 Sep 2026, as relayed by a third party [7].

# Detail

## Match format and objectives

| Element | Fact at Season 01 | Source |
|---------|-------------------|--------|
| Players | Up to 100 | [1][3] |
| Teams | 3 | [1][3] |
| Zone | Randomised 2x2 km Control Zone per match | [1] |
| Win condition | First team to 100 points | [1] |
| Scoring basis | Most players in zone earns points | [1] |
| Hot Zone | Double cash | [1] |
| Maps | 3, each with 3 variations | [3] |

Player-reported mechanics (not stated on the Steam page): a tick of roughly one point every 30 seconds; each player in the Hot Zone counts as two for presence; three to five tower capture points inside the Control Zone; completing a code from towers lets a team drag the Hot Zone toward a held tower [10][11]. Sources differ on exact wording, so treat these as player-reported.

## Local cross-team voice chat

The press release says local voice chat is audible across all three teams [3]. The Season 01 changelog as reported by press lists fixes to voice dropping after a failed join, to push-to-talk targeting and re-login recovery, plus a visible communication status [5].

## Progression and unlocks

| Element | Detail | Source |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Starting cash | $10,000 per life loadout budget | [1] |
| Cash earning | Teamplay actions, holding objective | [1] |
| Role tracks | Six tracks reported (Assault or Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot); naming differs between sources | [5][9] |
| Track cap | Level 100 per track and account level to 600, player-reported | [9] |
| Unlock cost | Reaching a level makes an item buyable for a one-time cash fee, player-reported | [9] |
| Season reset | Cash and XP reset; leftover cash converts to Gold Bars; Gold Bars and cosmetics persist | [7] |
| Per-season curve | "Each season we will have a different XP curve with different item orders" (quoted from the Season 1 changelog by a third party) | [7] |

Season 01 XP changes reported by press: level 10 rose from 25,000 to 35,000 XP, level 15 from 54,750 to 110,000, level 20 from 111,500 to 250,000, level 50 from 838,000 to 1,000,000; air vehicle kills pay 250 XP, down from 300 [5]. Economy abuse penalties (timed bans with cash and XP reset, permanent bans for XP farming) were reported in the 0.11 notes [4].

## Modes available at Early Access

The only official mode named is the Control Zone [1][3]. Official servers do not run modified game modes, while Community servers can [4]. The 0.1.2 Deploy flow points new players at Infantry mode and low-level community servers [6]. Assessment: a full catalogue of community-server modes could not be verified; confidence Low.

## Planned for 1.0

Developer statements: more maps, weapons and vehicles, deeper progression, a seasonal meta game and fighter jets [1]; continued work toward the wider meta game and long-term systems for 1.0 [3]. A third-party roadmap article additionally lists new objective types, a Black Market and Vault, and Player Skills and Challenges, but does not say which are official [8]. The developer reportedly will not publish a dated roadmap [8].
