# Executive Summary

WARDOGS is sold on Steam as two SKUs plus a separate DLC: the Standard game, the Supporter Edition bundle, and the Supporter Pack DLC [1][2]. On 2026-10-08 the Steam storefront API returned Standard at USD 39.99 and Supporter Edition at USD 49.99; the UK page shows GBP 36.99, GBP 45.99 and GBP 11.99 for the DLC [1][2]. The Supporter Pack is a list of cosmetics (camos, a scoreboard icon, two bobbleheads) and the developers state it is the only monetisation during Early Access [2][4].

The platform is Windows only. Linux is not officially supported and the developers say Proton compatibility work is ongoing without a timeline (statement of 25 Sep 2026) [7]. Xbox and PS5 are announced for "sometime in 2028" [5][6]. Sales were reported at over 1 million on 13 Sep and 3 million 18 days after launch [9][10].

Confidence is Medium. Prices and the DLC description are primary (Steam store data), but the sales, console and Linux claims rest on press reports of developer statements.

# Key Takeaways

- Steam SKUs: Standard, Supporter Edition (bundle of game plus Supporter Pack) and Supporter Pack DLC; UK prices GBP 36.99, 45.99 and 11.99 (checked 2026-10-08) [1].
- USD prices from the Steam API: Standard 39.99, Supporter Edition 49.99, Supporter Pack 12.99 (2026-10-08) [2].
- The Supporter Pack contents are three Starter Weapon camos, three branded weapon and vehicle camos, a scoreboard supporter icon and two bobbleheads; the cosmetic-only claim is the developer's own store text [2].
- The developers say Early Access is priced lower and the price may rise closer to full release [1][4].
- Windows is the only supported platform; Linux and Steam Deck are not supported, Proton work is "ongoing" (25 Sep 2026) [2][7].
- Console versions are "sometime in 2028"; no price, crossplay or edition detail is published [5][6].
- Sales milestones: over 1 million (reported 13 Sep), 3 million at 18 days (about 28 Sep 2026) [9][10].

# Current State

## Editions (checked 2026-10-08)

The Steam page lists WARDOGS, the Supporter Edition bundle ("includes WARDOGS and WARDOGS - Supporter Pack"), and the Supporter Pack as DLC [1]. The main page does not list the pack's contents; the DLC's own store entry does [1][2].

## Supporter Pack contents

The DLC entry says the pack "offers extra camos and cosmetic content" and lists three Starter Weapon camos, three branded weapon and vehicle camos, a scoreboard supporter icon and two bobbleheads [2]. The developer states there is no monetisation during Early Access beyond this optional pack, that in-game cash and gold bars must be earned through play, and that camos cannot be purchased directly [2]. The launch press release repeats that in-game cash, gold bars and status-based camouflage are not for sale [4]. Nothing in these sources lists a gameplay advantage, so the cosmetic-only claim is the developer's statement, not independently tested.

## Platforms

Steam lists Windows only (Windows 10 minimum, Windows 11 recommended) and the API reports no Mac or Linux support [1][2]. Xbox and PlayStation 5 versions are planned for "sometime in 2028"; Xbox Store wishlists were reported open on 10 Sep 2026 [5][6].

## Review snapshot

The Steam page showed "Mostly Positive", 79% of 63,179 English reviews, on 2026-10-08 (time of day not shown; the foundation recorded 63,152 earlier the same day) [1]. Push Square recorded "over 17,000" reviews, also "Mostly Positive", on 11 Sep 2026 [6].

## Early Access pricing statement

The Steam page: "we plan for WARDOGS to be priced lower during Early Access" and "we may raise the price closer to full release" [1]. The press release describes a tiered model rewarding earlier backers and gives USD 39.99 [4]. No full-release price or Early Access end date is published [4].

On 5 Sep 2026 the developers said "We do not officially support Linux and we cannot commit to having support for launch", that earlier Linux success in tests was by chance, and that buyers who expected Proton support could ask for a refund [7]. A developer comment dated 25 Sep 2026 (appearing in the reader comments of that article) says there is no new timeline and they will announce confirmed compatibility [7]. In August a developer said on the Steam forums they were working on Proton support [8]. No Steam Deck Verified rating was found [8]. Reports about which anti-cheat blocks Linux are unconfirmed [7].
