# Executive Summary

This document is a pre-Season-02 baseline of the non-weapon loadout items in WARDOGS: armor and helmets, tactical vests, backpacks, parachutes, ammo types, sidearms, optics, medical gadgets, throwables and IR gear. It goes deeper than the foundation document and is built so that later diffs against Season 02 are mechanical.

Official Steam posts publish very little item-level data. The official facts are a short list of Season 1 unlock-level and price changes (10 Sep 2026) [1], the rule that throwables cannot be used from enclosed vehicle seats (30 Sep 2026) [2], and the 2 Oct 2026 hotfix that disabled IR Rangefinders in the vendor until Season 02 [3]. Most item-level detail (armor, vests, medical costs, sidearm prices) is player-reported or from beta-era press, and is labelled so.

Confidence is Medium: the changes are official, but the item catalogue rests on player-reported guides and a beta-era press table.

# Key Takeaways

- Official, 2 Oct 2026: IR Rangefinders were disabled in the vendor until Season 02, when they will "require batteries to use"; the stated reason is that they were "too overpowered" [3].
- The same hotfix cut the CIWS time to destroy a Havoc from about 12 s to about 6 s [3].
- Official Season 1 (10 Sep 2026) moved Career unlocks Sports Parachute 36 to 35, Large Backpack 56 to 55, 762x54mm AP 83 to 82, 556mm AP 85 to 83 and Deagle 90 to 85 [1].
- Official: 155mm, 122mm and flare deployables take up more loadout space since 10 Sep 2026 [1].
- Official: grenades, smoke and C4 cannot be used from enclosed vehicle seats since 30 Sep 2026 [2].
- Official: Season 02 (15 Oct 2026) is described as bringing "new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks" [2][4].
- Player-reported: armor is not published officially; guides say a Level 2 Helmet guards against sniper fire and body armor covers the torso only [5].

# Scope

Covers every non-weapon loadout item with an official or guide mention, its price, unlock level, role, and patch changes up to the 2 Oct 2026 hotfix. Game version: Season 1, patch 0.1.2 plus hotfix. Excludes weapons (weapons-catalogue), damage and armor mathematics (damage-ballistics-and-balance), full unlock ladders (progression-unlock-tables) and full price lists (price-tables-season-1). Vehicles and buildables are in vehicles-outposts-foundation.

# Definitions

- **Career / Medic / Recon / Support / Driver / Pilot**: role tracks named in official patch notes as unlock groups [1].
- **WARDOG Level**: account level that, per a guide, handles general unlocks such as armor, backpacks, ammo types and sidearms (player-reported) [5].
- **One-time unlock cost vs vendor price**: a guide says an item is unlocked once for a payment, then bought per life at the vendor price (player-reported) [5].
- **IR Rangefinder**: the item named in the body of the 2 Oct hotfix; its title says "IR Goggles" [3].

# Current State

- IR Rangefinders were disabled in the vendor from the 2 Oct 2026 hotfix, rolling out as servers restart across regions within 12 hours; no client download or downtime needed [3].
- Official statement: they will return in Season 02 requiring batteries [3]. No battery capacity, price or unlock level has been published.
- CIWS now destroys the Havoc in about 6 seconds instead of 12, which Bulkhead describes as correcting an overcorrection in the previous patch [3].
- The Steam store page lists gear and a ghillie suit as available loadout content, with no item table [6].
- Throwables (grenades, smoke, C4) are barred from enclosed vehicle seats at patch 0.1.2 [2].

## Armor and helmets

No official armor values exist in the sources found. A guide describes a Level 2 Helmet as protection against sniper fire and body armor as protecting the torso only, with no price or unlock level (player-reported) [5]. The same guide says armor is unlocked via WARDOG Level [5].

## Tactical vests and backpacks

Player-reported: Small Tac Vest opens Quick Slots 5 and 6 with minor reload and equip speed bonuses; Medium opens 5 to 8 with larger bonuses; Large opens all slots [5]. Officially, a Large Backpack and a Sports Parachute exist as Career unlocks at levels 55 and 35 [1]. Small and medium backpack levels are not published.

## Parachutes

Official: Sports Parachute at Career 35 [1]. Player-reported: the Basic Parachute costs $100 and is single-use, lighter than the Sport Parachute [5].

## Ammo types

Official: 762x54mm AP (Career 82) and 556mm AP (Career 83) are unlockable ammo types [1]. Beta press: ammo was billed per caliber, roughly $10 to $250 a box [7]. The per-caliber table is not published.

## Sidearms

Official: Deagle at Career 85 [1]. Beta list prices are in the baseline table [7]; they predate the Season 1 repricing and may be stale.

## Optics and attachments

Official: Recon 6-10x MRAD and MOA scope prices rose 60% and 50% on 10 Sep 2026 [1]. Player-reported: attachments unlock by WARDOG or role level rather than direct purchase [7]; a compact red dot and a 6x Precision Rifle Scope are mentioned without prices [5].

## Medical gadgets and throwables

All figures are player-reported [5] (see baseline table). Official sources confirm only that revive validity is server authoritative (0.1.2) and that grenades, smoke and C4 exist as throwables [2].

## IR gear

IR Rangefinder is described by a boosting-seller blog as thermal goggles or binoculars that make enemies easier to spot (player-reported, conflict of interest) [8]. Price and unlock level are not stated in any source found.

# Risks & Caveats

- Season 02 is stated to bring new equipment and balance reworks [2]; this baseline will be largely superseded on 15 Oct 2026.
- The Season 1 post says each season has a different XP curve with different item orders [1], so levels here are Season 1 only.
- Most item-level numbers are player-reported and some are beta-era.
- The boosting-seller source [8] has a conflict of interest.

# Open Questions

- Naming conflict: the 2 Oct post title says "IR Goggles" but the body says "IR Rangefinders" [3]. Whether these are one item is unverified.
- Source-ID conflict: the foundation document cites the Season 1 changelog at gid 701027323413004455; the saved snapshot lists it as gid 1843481262690549 [1].
- Official vs foundation: the foundation lists "Large Hammer $25,000 to $75,000" as a vendor-style repricing; official text lists it as the Support price, with a separate vendor price of $1,600 to $2,400 [1].
- Official text says "Sports Parachute"; guides say "Sport Parachute" and "Basic Parachute" [1][5].
- Official armor values, tactical vest levels, battery mechanics and IR gear price remain unpublished; Season 02 notes should resolve them.
