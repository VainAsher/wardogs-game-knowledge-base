# Executive Summary

This document covers the non-weapon loadout items in WARDOGS: armor and helmets, backpacks, parachutes, ammo types, sidearms, optics, medical gadgets, throwables and IR gear. Season 1 (patch 0.1.2 plus the 2 Oct 2026 hotfix) is kept as dated history; the current state is Update 0.2.0 (Season 02), whose official patch notes were posted 14 Oct 2026 [9] and which the developer announced as released on 15 Oct 2026 [10].

Update 0.2.0 is the first official source to publish item-level numbers: Level 3 armor and helmet damage reduction lowered 55% to 50%, Level 4 lowered 65% to 60%, Level 3 and Level 4 helmets reducing suppression by 68% and 80%, a Level 4 Helmet with muffled audio, muffled proximity chat, restricted field of view and sprint fog-over, a battery-powered Infrared Range Finder (about 80 s per battery), a new Molotov Cocktail, Medical Bag durability 100 to 150, a parachute rework and a 30mm-ammo 2x2 slot with a 50-item backpack stack cap [9]. No official post after 2026-10-15 is in the 2026-10-30 snapshot, so later hotfixes are neither confirmed nor ruled out.

Confidence is Medium: the 0.2.0 changes are Primary-sourced, but the underlying item catalogue (for example sidearm base prices and medical-gadget details) still rests on player-reported guides and a beta-era press table.

# Key Takeaways

- Update 0.2.0 (notes 14 Oct 2026): Level 3 Armor and Helmet damage reduction 55% to 50%; Level 4 65% to 60% [9]. Ballistics context is in damage-ballistics-and-balance.
- Level 3 helmets reduce suppression by 68% and Level 4 helmets by 80% (0.2.0) [9].
- Level 4 Helmet drawbacks (0.2.0): muffled audio, muffled proximity chat, restricted field of view, fogs over when sprinting [9].
- The Infrared Range Finder now runs on batteries: about 80 s of active use per battery, draining faster if toggled on and off quickly (0.2.0) [9]. Its vendor price rose $1,200 to $3,200 [9]. It had been disabled in the vendor by the 2 Oct 2026 hotfix [3]; the 0.2.0 notes do not say whether it is purchasable at launch.
- New Molotov Cocktail (Assault level 13, $25k unlock, $340 vendor price): impact damage plus burn damage over time, throw overarm or underarm, burns destructible foliage, weaker in rain (0.2.0) [9].
- Medical Bag durability 100 to 150; unlock moved Medic level 24 to 14 and $75k to $50k; vendor price $2,000 to $1,800 (0.2.0) [9].
- Parachutes (0.2.0): longer to open, will not open below about 10 m, and opening below roughly 50 m while falling fast means fall damage; Basic Parachute $100 to $500, Sport Parachute $1,000 to $700 [9].
- Explosives and placeables can no longer be used from enclosed vehicle seats (0.2.0), extending the 0.1.2 throwables rule [2][9].

# Scope

Covers every non-weapon loadout item with an official or guide mention, its price, unlock level, role, and patch changes up to Update 0.2.0 (15 Oct 2026). Excludes weapons (weapons-catalogue), damage and armor mathematics (damage-ballistics-and-balance), full unlock ladders (progression-unlock-tables) and full price lists (price-tables-season-1, which owns vendor prices). Vehicles and buildables are in vehicles-outposts-foundation.

# Definitions

- **Assault / Medic / Recon / Support / Pilot / Driver / WARDOG Progression Track**: the track headings used in the 0.2.0 notes [9]. Season 1 notes named Career, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot [1]; what happened to "Career" is not stated.
- **WARDOG Level**: guide term for general unlocks such as armor, backpacks, ammo types and sidearms (player-reported) [5]; the 0.2.0 notes use the heading "WARDOG Progression Track" for these unlocks [9].
- **One-time unlock cost vs vendor price**: a guide says an item is unlocked once for a payment, then bought per life at the vendor price (player-reported) [5]; the 0.2.0 notes list "to unlock" and vendor prices separately [9].
- **Infrared Range Finder / IR Rangefinder**: the 0.2.0 notes say "Infrared Range Finder" [9]; the 2 Oct hotfix title says "IR Goggles" and its body "IR Rangefinders" [3].

# Current State

All items below apply to Update 0.2.0 (notes 14 Oct 2026, release 15 Oct 2026) unless dated otherwise [9][10].

- Armor and helmets: Level 3 damage reduction 50% and Level 4 damage reduction 60% [9]. Against a Level 1 helmet a Scout Rifle needs two headshots; with no helmet one headshot kills [9].
- Helmet suppression resistance: Level 3 reduces suppression by 68%, Level 4 by 80% [9].
- Level 4 Helmet: muffled audio, restricted field of view, fogs over when sprinting; the Combat Balance section also lists muffled proximity chat [9].
- Infrared Range Finder: batteries, about 80 s of active use per battery, drains faster if toggled quickly [9]. Battery price and recharge rules are not stated.
- Molotov Cocktail: Assault track level 13, $25k to unlock, $340 vendor price [9].
- Medical Bag: durability 150, Medic level 14 [9].
- Sport Parachute unlock level 35 to 34 (WARDOG track) [9].
- 30mm cannon ammo takes a 2x2 inventory slot instead of 1x1; backpack stacks are capped at 50; vehicle storage stacks stay at 200 [9].
- Explosives and placeables are barred from enclosed vehicle seats [9]. Barbed wire no longer gives cash or XP on kills [9].
- Halligan Bar now breaches both doors and gates; uses raised 5 to 7; vendor price $1,100 to $885 [9].
- Rain reduces Molotov fire and affects spotting range [9].
- The Steam store page lists gear and a ghillie suit as loadout content, with no item table (live capture 2026-10-08) [6].
- No official post after 2026-10-15 is in the 2026-10-30 snapshot.

## Armor and helmets

Season 1 had no official armor values; a guide described a Level 2 Helmet as protection against sniper fire and body armor as protecting the torso only (player-reported) [5]. Update 0.2.0 is the first official source: Level 3 damage reduction 55% to 50% and Level 4 65% to 60% (armor and helmets), Level 3 and Level 4 helmets with increased suppression resistance (68% and 80% reductions), and a Level 4 Helmet with muffled audio, muffled proximity chat, restricted field of view and sprint fog-over [9]. The notes frame the helmet changes as letting these items better fulfil their protective role in more ways than damage reduction [9]. The Level 3 Helmet & Armor unlock moved to level 70 [9]. Also in 0.2.0: Scout Rifle headshots one-shot no-helmet targets and need two against a Level 1 helmet; 12 gauge slug and compound bow damage against armor scale with armor tier [9]. Level 2 values are not stated. Absolute damage numbers are not published; see damage-ballistics-and-balance.

## Tactical vests and backpacks

Player-reported (Season 1): Small Tac Vest opens Quick Slots 5 and 6 with minor reload and equip speed bonuses; Medium opens 5 to 8 with larger bonuses; Large opens all slots [5]. The 0.2.0 notes do not mention vests. Backpack changes in 0.2.0: Ruck, Gunner (+ Sling) and Arsenal (+ 2 Slings) prices fell (see table); backpack stacks are capped at 50; 30mm cannon ammo takes a 2x2 slot; vehicle storage stacks stay at 200 [9]. Season 1: Large Backpack was a Career unlock at level 55 [1]; the notes do not restate its level.

## Parachutes

Update 0.2.0 [9]: parachutes take longer to fully open and will not open at all below about 10 m; opening below roughly 50 m while falling fast means fall damage; Basic Parachute $100 to $500; Sport Parachute $1,000 to $700; Sport Parachute unlock level 35 to 34. The notes add a warning to trust a pilot before boarding their aircraft. Season 1: Sports Parachute Career 35 [1]; Basic Parachute $100, single use, lighter than the Sport Parachute (player-reported) [5]. Official text says "Sports Parachute" (Season 1) and "Sport Parachute" (0.2.0) [1][9].

## Ammo types

0.2.0 unlock-level changes in the WARDOG track [9]: 12g Rifled Slug 41 to 36, 7.62x39mm AP 63 to 60, .308 Win Flesh Damage (HP) 65 to 63, 5.45x39mm HP 16 to 6, 9x19mm HP 6 to 10, 5.56mm HP 10 to 13, 5.56mm AP 83 to 84, 5.45x39mm AP 93 to 90. Season 1: 762x54mm AP (Career 82) and 556mm AP (Career 83) [1]; beta press: ammo billed per caliber, roughly $10 to $250 a box [7]. Calibre base-damage reductions (.308 -25%, 7.62x54mmR -20%, 7.62x39mm -19%, .50 Cal -16%) are in damage-ballistics-and-balance [9]. A per-caliber ammo price table is not published in the notes.

## Sidearms

0.2.0 [9]: Judge level 18 to 1 and $15k to $5k; GGX 17 level 1 to 18 and $5k to $15k; Deagle level 85 to 65 and vendor price $900 to $750; GGX 18 level 70 to 85, unlock $50k to $125k, vendor price $800 to $1,000. Season 1: Deagle at Career 85 [1]; beta list prices are in the Season 1 table and may be stale [7].

## Optics and attachments

0.2.0 adds optics, muzzle devices, magazines and bipods alongside the four new weapons [9]. New attachments and vendor prices [9]: Compact P-1 Red Dot ($480; WARDOG level 21, $12.5k), PU 91/30 Scope for the Mosin ($780; Recon level 10, $12.5k), Leatherwood ART II for the M14 ($950), Evolys Bipod for the Evo-3 Ultra ($1,450), M14 Bipod ($1,480), Cross Rifle Muzzle Brake ($780), Clutch Lok Muzzle Brake ($1,100) and SRD762TI-QD Suppressor ($1,350) for the TCX-SPR, SL7i-BL Suppressor for the Evo-3 Ultra ($1,400), .30 Cal SS Phosphate Muzzle Brake ($950) and .30 Cal Steel Black Flash Hider ($730) for the M14. New magazines: PP-19 Bizon 53 RND $200, TCX-SPR 28 RND $50, Evolys 50 RND Box $110, M14 10, 20 and 30 RND $60, $110 and $180 [9]. The Mosin loses access to other optics [9]. The notes say most attachments moved to earlier levels or new tracks; the full lists are in progression-unlock-tables and price-tables-season-1 [9]. Season 1: Recon 6-10x MRAD and MOA scope prices rose 60% and 50% [1]; a compact red dot and a 6x Precision Rifle Scope were mentioned by a guide without prices (player-reported) [5].

## Medical gadgets and throwables

Update 0.2.0 [9]: Medical Bag durability 100 to 150 (a single bag goes further before replacement), unlock Medic 14 and $50k, price $1,800; Defibrillator unlock Medic 22 and $110k; First Aid Kit level 10; Adrenaline Pen level 16; M18 Smoke Grenade: Black level 23; Field Resuscitator price $425. The guide's "Emergency Resuscitator" and the notes' "Field Resuscitator" may be the same item; this is not stated. Molotov Cocktail is a thrown incendiary that splashes fire on impact, deals impact damage plus damage over time to anyone standing in the flames, can be thrown overarm or underarm, burns destructible foliage and is reduced by rain; Assault level 13, $25k unlock, $340 [9]. C4 and the Remote Detonator moved into the Assault track (level 4), and IED/C4 now give assault XP [9]. Explosives and placeables are barred from enclosed vehicle seats [9]; the 0.1.2 rule covered grenades, smoke and C4 [2]. Barbed wire gives no cash or XP on kills [9]. Season 1 player-reported prices are in the baseline table [5].

## IR gear

Update 0.2.0 [9]: the Infrared Range Finder runs on batteries, about 80 s of active use per battery, draining faster if quickly toggled on and off; vendor price $1,200 to $3,200. The plain Range Finder is a separate priced item ($400 to $1,450; Recon unlock $15k to $25k) [9]. The 2 Oct 2026 hotfix had disabled IR Rangefinders in the vendor, calling them "too overpowered" and promising batteries [3]. Whether the item is purchasable at the 0.2.0 launch, its unlock level and battery cost are not stated. A boosting-seller blog describes IR gear as thermal goggles or binoculars that ease spotting (player-reported, conflict of interest) [8].

# Risks & Caveats

- The 0.2.0 values are as of 14-15 Oct 2026; later hotfixes, if any, are not in the 2026-10-30 snapshot.
- The developer says each season has a different XP curve and item order [1], and 0.2.0 moved many levels; Season 1 levels are history only.
- Sidearm, vest and several medical-gadget details beyond the notes rest on player-reported or beta-era sources.
- The Season 02 preview used different item names for some content; this document uses the final names from the patch notes, for example "Level 4 Helmet" [9].
- The boosting-seller source [8] has a conflict of interest.

# Open Questions

- Whether the Infrared Range Finder is purchasable at the 0.2.0 launch, its unlock level, and battery cost, capacity and recharge: the notes state only about 80 s per battery and a $3,200 price [9].
- Naming: "IR Goggles" (2 Oct post title) versus "IR Rangefinders" versus "Infrared Range Finder" [3][9]; whether all are one item is unverified.
- Whether "Emergency Resuscitator" (guide) and "Field Resuscitator" (notes) are the same item [5][9].
- Official absolute armor values, Level 2 values and tactical vest levels remain unpublished.
- The Level 4 Helmet's muffled proximity chat appears in the Combat Balance section but not in the Helmets Update summary; whether it applies to all voice is not stated [9].
- Official vs foundation: the Season 1 Large Hammer $25,000 to $75,000 is the Support unlock price, with a separate vendor price of $1,600 to $2,400 [1].
- Whether Season 02 reset any progression: the notes do not mention it.
