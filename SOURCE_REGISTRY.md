# Source Registry

## Ranked source authority

1. Steam store page (app 1867240), Steam news and official patch notes.
2. Bulkhead and Team17 official sources: website, press releases, developer posts, official announcements.
3. In-game verifiable data and official documentation, including server docs.
4. Official wiki (if one exists) and community wikis, flagged as community.
5. Reputable press (PCGamesN, Pure Xbox, GamesBeat, Push Square) for release and business facts.
6. Community guides, forums, Reddit and videos. Player-reported evidence only.

## Classes

Primary (ranks 1-3) / Press & Secondary (5) / Player-Reported (4, 6).
