# Glossary

| Term | Definition | Source |
|------|------------|--------|
| WARDOGS | 100-player, 3-team tactical FPS by Bulkhead, published by Team17 | steam-store |
| Early Access | Steam pre-release state; WARDOGS entered it 10 Sept 2026 | team17-press |
| Control Zone | Randomised 2x2 km objective area per match; team with most players inside scores, first to 100 wins | steam-store |
| Hot Zone | Smaller area where presence and cash count double (player-reported) | allthings-hotzone |
| Role track | Per-role XP track gating unlocks; count and names differ by source (player-reported) | allthings-roles |
| Season | Period after which cash and XP reset; cash-to-Gold-Bar conversion rests on a single commercial source and is unverified | timesaver-s2 |
| Loadout | Weapons, gear, utility and vehicles bought with cash at the vendor for one life | steam-store |
| WARDOG Level | Account-wide level unlocking general items (player-reported) | keengamer-progression |
| Re-buy Loadout | Vendor option rebuilding the previous kit after death (player-reported) | keengamer-progression |
| Cash | In-game currency earned by teamplay and spent on per-life loadouts; persists between matches; not purchasable | steam-store |
| Account balance | Persistent cash total that match outcomes feed into | team17-press |
| Gold Bars | Second currency named in the Team17 press release as not purchasable; seasonal role unverified | team17-press |
